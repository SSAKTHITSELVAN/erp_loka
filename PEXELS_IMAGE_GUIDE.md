## Download Steps

1. **Create directory** (if it doesn't exist):
   ```bash
   mkdir -p /home/sakthi-selvan/web/erp_loka/src/assets/images/about
   ```

2. **For each image**:
   - Search on Pexels with the term
   - Choose a landscape-oriented image (1920x1080 or similar)
   - Click "Free Download" → Choose "Large" or "Original"
   - Save to: `/home/sakthi-selvan/web/erp_loka/src/assets/images/about/`
   - Rename to exact filename above

3. **Image Tips**:
   - Choose images with **subtle backgrounds** (not too busy)
   - Prefer images with **blue tones** to match brand
   - Avoid images with text/logos
   - Landscape orientation works best
   - Resolution: At least 1920px wide
